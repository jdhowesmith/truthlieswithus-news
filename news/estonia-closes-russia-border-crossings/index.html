Estonia to close all five border crossings with Russia for six months

The closure starts Dec. 1 and is for security reasons, Reuters reports.

By Truth Lies With Us Staff

Fri, Nov 20, 2026, 7:35 a.m. B.C. time · 1 min read

The facts at a glance

  • Estonia will close all five border crossings with Russia starting Dec. 1.
  • The closure is for at least six months.
  • Estonia cited security.
The reporting

Estonia will close all five of its border crossings with Russia for at least six months starting Dec. 1, citing security, Reuters reports.

The announcement came as Russia carried out one of the largest air attacks of the war on Ukraine overnight, and as Poland said Russian missiles and a drone crossed into its airspace.

Analysis

Our Take

Coming the same day missiles crossed into Poland, this shows how NATO's eastern members are hardening their borders with Russia.
