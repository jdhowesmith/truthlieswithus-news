24:T748,<p>The Tampa Bay Lightning beat the Edmonton Oilers 3-1 on Thursday night at Benchmark International Arena, ending Edmonton&#39;s six-game winning streak and handing the Oilers their first regulation road loss of the season.</p>
<h2>How it happened</h2>
<p>John Carlson opened the scoring on a power play at 13:29 of the second period, with Evan Bouchard in the penalty box for tripping. Ilya Mikheyev made it 2-0 at 10:46 of the third.</p>
<p>The third goal, at 14:29, was credited to Nikita Kucherov. His breakaway shot was trickling through goalie Devon Levi when Mattias Ekholm accidentally knocked it in while trying to clear it. Colton Dach scored for Edmonton at 18:24 with Levi pulled for an extra attacker.</p>
<p>Levi made 16 saves on 19 shots. Andrei Vasilevskiy stopped 23 of 24 for Tampa Bay. The official summary has shots 24-19 for Edmonton, with the Oilers 0-for-3 on the power play and the Lightning 1-for-2.</p>
<h2>Streaks end</h2>
<p>Connor McDavid&#39;s season-opening point streak ended at 23 games; one more point would have given him sole possession of fourth place all-time. Leon Draisaitl&#39;s 12-game point streak and Levi&#39;s personal six-game winning streak also ended. Edmonton is 17-4-3 and 10-1-1 on the road.</p>
<h2>What they said</h2>
<p>&quot;I actually thought it was a really good game, real good teams,&quot; coach Mike Babcock told NHL.com. &quot;In the end they were better than us and capitalized on their opportunities.&quot;</p>
<p>Ekholm took the blame for the third goal: &quot;That&#39;s my bad. I&#39;ll get Levs a nice dinner for that one.&quot;</p>
<h2>Injuries and next game</h2>
<p>Ryan Nugent-Hopkins (illness) missed a third straight game; the team hasn&#39;t given a return date. Edmonton plays at Carolina on Saturday at 11 a.m. B.C. time (Sportsnet), then at St. Louis on Sunday at 5 p.m. B.C. time.</p>
