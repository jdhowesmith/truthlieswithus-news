Lorne Doerkson sworn in today as B.C.'s 38th premier

The Conservative leader and his cabinet take their oaths at Government House in Victoria at 11 a.m. B.C. time. There will be no fall sitting of the legislature.

By Truth Lies With Us Staff

Fri, Nov 20, 2026, 7:44 a.m. B.C. time · 1 min read

The facts at a glance

  • Doerkson is sworn in as B.C.'s 38th premier at Government House in Victoria at 11 a.m. B.C. time, with his Conservative cabinet.
  • On Thursday, 63 of the 64 Conservative MLAs took their oaths of office.
  • Kelowna Centre's Kristina Loewen is waiting for a judicial recount; she leads by 43 votes.
  • Green MLAs are sworn in Nov. 23 and NDP MLAs Nov. 26.
  • Doerkson says there will be no fall sitting of the legislature.
The reporting

Lorne Doerkson becomes British Columbia's 38th premier today. He and his Conservative cabinet are to be sworn in at Government House in Victoria at 11 a.m. B.C. time.

On Thursday, 63 of the 64 Conservative MLAs took their oaths of office. The one still waiting is Kelowna Centre's Kristina Loewen, whose seat is going to a judicial recount; she leads by 43 votes.

What happens next

Green MLAs are scheduled to be sworn in Nov. 23 and NDP MLAs on Nov. 26. Doerkson has said there will be no fall sitting of the legislature, so the new government's first session will come later.

Analysis

Our Take

Today makes the change of government official. The cabinet list is the first real signal of priorities, so watch who gets finance, health and the rural portfolios that matter in the Shuswap. With no fall sitting, the new government's agenda won't face the legislature until the new year.
