Ottawa developing Arctic military satellite communications to cut reliance on Starlink

CBC reports Canada is working on Arctic-ready military satellite communications so it depends less on Elon Musk's Starlink.

By Truth Lies With Us Staff

Fri, Nov 20, 2026, 7:33 a.m. B.C. time · 1 min read

XFacebookEmail

The facts at a glance

  • Canada is developing Arctic-ready military satellite communications, CBC reports.
  • The goal is to reduce reliance on Elon Musk's Starlink.
The reporting

Canada is developing Arctic-ready military satellite communications to reduce its reliance on Elon Musk's Starlink network, CBC News reports.

Reliable communications across the Arctic are a long-standing challenge for the Canadian Armed Forces, and the move would give the military a Canadian-controlled option in the North.

Analysis

Our Take

Relying on one private company's network for military communications in the North is a sovereignty risk. Building a Canadian option takes years and money, so watch for budgets and timelines.
