Brooke Henderson opens with 74 at the CME Group Tour Championship

The Canadian is 2 over par and tied for 57th in the 60-player field, nine shots behind a three-way lead at Tiburón in Naples.

By Truth Lies With Us Staff

Fri, Nov 20, 2026, 7:42 a.m. B.C. time · 1 min read

XFacebookEmail

The facts at a glance

  • Henderson shot 74 (39-35) in Thursday's first round and is tied for 57th with Erika Hara and Jenny Shin.
  • Jeeno Thitikul, A Lim Kim and Karis Davidson share the lead at 7-under 65.
  • Nelly Korda shot 68; a win would put her in the LPGA Hall of Fame.
  • Henderson teed off in Round 2 at 5:55 a.m. B.C. time with Lydia Ko.
  • The event has no cut, a US$11 million purse and US$4 million to the winner.
The reporting

Brooke Henderson shot a 2-over-par 74 in the first round of the CME Group Tour Championship at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Fla., and is tied for 57th in the 60-player field.

Henderson went out in 39 and came home in 35. Only Lydia Ko, at 77, is lower. Henderson is tied with Erika Hara and Jenny Shin, nine shots behind the leaders.

The leaders

Defending champion Jeeno Thitikul, who birdied her last five holes, A Lim Kim, and Australian Karis Davidson, in her first CME start, share the lead at 7-under 65. Nine players are one back at 66, including Charley Hull and Ayaka Furue. Nelly Korda shot 68; a win would put her in the LPGA Hall of Fame. Forty-nine of the 60 players broke par.

Round 2

Henderson teed off at 5:55 a.m. B.C. time in the first group, off the first tee, with Ko. Golf Channel coverage starts at noon B.C. time, after her round is likely to finish, so follow her on the LPGA leaderboard.

Analysis

Our Take

Nine shots back with 54 holes to play is a big hole in a field where 49 of 60 players broke par. With no cut, Henderson gets all four rounds, and a low round today would move her well up the leaderboard and the money list.
