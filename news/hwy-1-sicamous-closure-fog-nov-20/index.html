Weather & Alerts· Sicamous, B.C.

Highway 1 closed near Sicamous 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.; fog patches east of Chase

DriveBC lists a daytime closure near Sicamous today, BC Hydro work on Highway 97A, and fog on the Trans-Canada between Chase and Sicamous this morning.

By Truth Lies With Us Staff

Fri, Nov 20, 2026, 7:36 a.m. B.C. time · 1 min read

XFacebookEmail

The facts at a glance

  • Highway 1 between Old Sicamous Rd and Solsqua-Sicamous Rd is closed 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. today; use Highways 97A/97B.
  • Fog patches on Highway 1 between Waverly Park (east of Chase) and Bernie Road (west of Sicamous).
  • Sicamous to Salmon Arm: single-lane alternating traffic for BC Hydro pole work, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. weekdays until Nov. 30.
  • Highway 97A, Seed Rd to Davy Rd: BC Hydro work today 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., single-lane alternating.
  • No closures listed for Rogers Pass.
The reporting

DriveBC lists a daytime closure on the Trans-Canada Highway near Sicamous today. Highway 1 between Old Sicamous Road and Solsqua-Sicamous Road is closed from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.; drivers should use Highways 97A and 97B.

There were fog patches this morning on Highway 1 between Waverly Park, east of Chase, and Bernie Road, west of Sicamous.

Other work

  • Sicamous to Salmon Arm: BC Hydro pole work with single-lane alternating traffic, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays until Nov. 30.
  • Highway 97A, Seed Road to Davy Road: BC Hydro work today from 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., single-lane alternating traffic.
  • Kamloops: Highway 1 from Highland Road to Tanager Drive, right lane closed both ways 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; today is the last day.

No closures were listed for Rogers Pass (checked about 7:30 a.m. B.C. time).

Analysis

Our Take

If you're heading east past Sicamous today, plan around the midday closure. The 97A/97B detour has its own BC Hydro work, so leave extra time.
