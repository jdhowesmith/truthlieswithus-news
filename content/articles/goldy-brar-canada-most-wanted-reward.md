Police are offering a reward of up to $250,000 for information leading to the arrest of Satinderjeet Singh, known as Goldy Brar, who has been named Canada's most-wanted fugitive.

The reward is offered through the BOLO Program together with the Winnipeg Police Service. It covers an arrest in Canada or the United States and runs until Nov. 2, 2027.

Police describe Brar as a top figure in the Bishnoi gang. He is wanted in connection with a 2023 killing in Winnipeg.

Anyone with information can call the tip line at 431-334-9160.
