The Edmonton Oilers play the Tampa Bay Lightning tonight at 5:30 p.m. B.C. time (7:30 p.m. ET) at Benchmark International Arena. TV in Canada is Sportsnet West; radio is 880 CHED.

Edmonton is 17-3-3 (37 points), first in the Pacific Division and first in the NHL, and has the league's best road record at 10-0-1. Tampa Bay is 14-5-2 (30 points), first in the Atlantic, with the best home record at 9-1-0. The Oilers are going for a seventh straight win, but Oilers.com notes they are 1-10-2 in their last 13 visits to Tampa, going back to 2011-12. Tampa won both games last season.

## Streaks on the line

According to Oilers.com, Connor McDavid's season-opening point streak is 23 games, and one point tonight would give him sole possession of fourth place all-time. Evan Bouchard can tie Paul Coffey's NHL record for the longest season-opening road point streak by a defenceman. Leon Draisaitl's point streak is 10 games.

## Lineup and injuries

No official lineup was out as of about 7 a.m. B.C. time. Devon Levi has won six straight starts, but tonight's starter isn't confirmed. Ryan Nugent-Hopkins (illness, per the Edmonton Journal) has missed two straight games, with no update yet. Frederik Andersen, Mattias Janmark, Matt Savoie and Alec Regula remain on injured reserve.

Isaac Howard was activated off injured reserve and loaned to AHL Bakersfield on Monday, and scored in his AHL season debut Wednesday night, the Condors' only goal in a 7-1 loss at San Diego.

For Tampa Bay, Brandon Hagel (lower body) is day to day, Conor Geekie went on injured reserve, and Nikita Kucherov leads the team with 30 points, according to NHL.com and Lightning.com.

## Next up

Saturday at Carolina, 11 a.m. B.C. time (Sportsnet), and Sunday at St. Louis, 5 p.m. B.C. time.
