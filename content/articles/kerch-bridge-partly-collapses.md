A key bridge linking occupied Crimea to mainland Russia has partly collapsed after Ukraine attacked it for the second night in a row, dealing a major symbolic and military blow to Vladimir Putin.

Photos and video geolocated by NBC News show one section of the Kerch Bridge fallen into the water. Ukraine's security service, the SBU, said one of the bridge's metal arches and the road span beneath it were "completely destroyed" and that the rail section was also damaged. The new strike came shortly after midnight Tuesday local time, which was about 2 p.m. Monday in B.C.

## Traffic halted, a terrorism case opened

Russia's Transport Ministry said all road and rail traffic on the bridge was suspended while experts assessed the damage. Later on Tuesday, the Russian-installed head of Crimea, Sergey Aksyonov, said some rail service had resumed and that ferries were taking passengers without vehicles. He also said two people were killed in a drone strike in a district near the bridge.

Russia's Investigative Committee confirmed "damage to the structure" and opened a criminal case it called a terrorist act. Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said a plan would be drawn up and the bridge would be restored, "even better than before." Putin himself had not commented publicly.

## Why this bridge matters

Putin opened the 19-kilometre (12-mile) road and rail bridge himself in 2018, four years after Russia illegally annexed Crimea. It cost about US$2.7 billion and has been a main supply route for Russian forces on the peninsula, as well as a crossing for civilians. It also spans the channel ships use to move between the Sea of Azov and the Black Sea, so fallen sections could block that route for some time.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy called it "an unprecedented operation in response to Russian strikes on our infrastructure," and said it was the seventh stage of a weeks-long campaign that first targeted air defences and radars along the approach.

## Russia strikes back at Kyiv

Overnight, Russian missiles damaged Kyiv's Northern and Southern bridges over the Dnipro River, and both were closed. Kyiv officials said two people were killed. Russia's Defence Ministry said it had shot down more than 1,400 Ukrainian drones, the largest such attack of the war by its count.

## The summit backdrop

The strikes come just before Thursday's meeting of U.S. President Donald Trump, China's Xi Jinping and Putin in Shenzhen, China. Trump said Monday that Ukraine would be discussed. U.S.-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi last week ended without a breakthrough.
