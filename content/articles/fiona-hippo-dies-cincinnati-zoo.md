Fiona the hippo, the Cincinnati Zoo's most famous resident, has died at 9, the zoo announced.

The zoo says Fiona died of complications after swallowing a knit hat that blew into her enclosure.

Fiona became an internet favourite after she was born premature in 2017, and the zoo's updates on her care drew a following around the world.
