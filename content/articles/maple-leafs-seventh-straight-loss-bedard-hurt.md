Frank Nazar and Tyler Bertuzzi each scored twice, and the Chicago Blackhawks handed the Toronto Maple Leafs their seventh straight loss, 6-3 on Tuesday night. Chicago lost Connor Bedard to an injury in the third period.

Chicago won. Toronto lost. The Maple Leafs' slide is their longest since March 2015, according to The Associated Press. Toronto is 8-13-2.

## Bedard hurt after go-ahead goal

Bedard gave Chicago a 3-2 lead it would not give up, scoring his seventh goal late in the second period (18:51, according to the NHL scoring summary). He then left abruptly after trying to take a faceoff 54 seconds into the third. The AP reported that his right hand was struck by the stick of Toronto's Nick Paul, and that he yelled out before skating straight to the bench and locker room with his hand raised.

The AP noted Bedard hurt his right shoulder on a faceoff against St. Louis last December and missed nearly a month, and had left shoulder surgery in the off-season.

## How it happened

- Easton Cowan opened the scoring for Toronto 3:22 into the game.
- Nazar scored twice in the second period, first on the power play. Teddy Blueger tied it 2-2 before Bedard's goal.
- Chicago put it away with three goals in 3:03 in the third: Bertuzzi on the power play, Oliver Moore, then Bertuzzi again. John Tavares scored for Toronto in between.

Spencer Knight stopped 31 shots for Chicago, and defenceman Sam Rinzel had three assists. Sergei Bobrovsky made 19 saves on 25 shots for Toronto.

The Blackhawks came in with the NHL's third-worst power play, at 14.3%, but went 2-for-3. They have not lost in regulation in three games, their longest such stretch this season.

## A century-old rivalry

The Blackhawks marked the 100th anniversary of the franchise's first game, which was played against the Toronto St. Pats. A few months later, that team changed its name to the Maple Leafs, the AP noted.

**Next:** Toronto plays Thursday in Los Angeles. Chicago hosts Columbus on Thursday.
