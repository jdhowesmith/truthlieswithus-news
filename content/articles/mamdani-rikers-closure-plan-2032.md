New York City Mayor Zohran Mamdani has laid out a plan to close the Rikers Island jail complex. It would also push the closing back by about five years.

Under the plan, four borough jails would open: Brooklyn in 2029, the Bronx and Queens in 2031, and Manhattan in 2032. The city would ask City Council to move the legal closure deadline from August 2027 to the end of 2032.

The plan also aims to cut the jail population from about 6,600 to 4,100.
