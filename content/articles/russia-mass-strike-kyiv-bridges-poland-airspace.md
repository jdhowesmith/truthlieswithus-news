Russia launched one of the largest air attacks of the war on Ukraine overnight, hitting three bridges over the Dnipro River in Kyiv and knocking out power across wide areas, Ukrainian officials said. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Russia fired more than 400 drones and over 110 missiles, 27 of them ballistic.

It was the biggest attack on Kyiv's bridges since Russia's full-scale invasion in 2022.

## Casualties and damage

Prime Minister Sergii Koretskyi said eight people were killed, six in Kyiv and two at a railway station in Lviv in western Ukraine. People were also hurt in the Dnipropetrovsk region and in Kharkiv, where drones hit a hospital. Rail lines were damaged and power was knocked out widely.

Final casualty counts were not yet known Friday morning, and Reuters said it could not immediately confirm all the reports. The Kyiv Independent, counting attacks over the past day across the country, reported 12 killed and 82 injured.

## Missiles cross into Poland

Poland's defence minister said two missiles, one of them a Kh-101 cruise missile, and a drone crossed into Polish airspace, and Polish jets were scrambled. Prime Minister Donald Tusk said the incursion "does not look like an accident" and called it "an intentional provocation." Poland will ask NATO for more support on its eastern flank.

## Why now

Vladimir Putin had vowed to respond after Ukraine struck the Crimean (Kerch) Bridge again earlier this week. Ukraine also says it hit a pumping station on the Druzhba oil pipeline in Tatarstan on Thursday.

## Estonia closes its border

Separately, Estonia said it will close all five of its border crossings with Russia for at least six months starting Dec. 1, citing security, according to Reuters.
