DriveBC lists a daytime closure on the Trans-Canada Highway near Sicamous today. Highway 1 between Old Sicamous Road and Solsqua-Sicamous Road is closed from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m.; drivers should use Highways 97A and 97B.

There were fog patches this morning on Highway 1 between Waverly Park, east of Chase, and Bernie Road, west of Sicamous.

## Other work

- Sicamous to Salmon Arm: BC Hydro pole work with single-lane alternating traffic, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. on weekdays until Nov. 30.
- Highway 97A, Seed Road to Davy Road: BC Hydro work today from 9:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., single-lane alternating traffic.
- Kamloops: Highway 1 from Highland Road to Tanager Drive, right lane closed both ways 8 a.m. to 8 p.m.; today is the last day.

No closures were listed for Rogers Pass (checked about 7:30 a.m. B.C. time).
