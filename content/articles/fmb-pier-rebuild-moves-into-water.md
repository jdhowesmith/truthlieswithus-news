Work on the new Fort Myers Beach pier is moving off the sand and into the Gulf, according to a Lee County update shared Thursday.

Pile driving is shifting from the beach to the water. A barge is on site, buoys mark the work zone, and work may take place on weekends. The county asks people to stay clear of the barge and the marked area. Flaggers may briefly hold beachgoers while piles are loaded.

The pedestrian detour on Estero Boulevard has been removed.

The pier work is happening during a busy stretch on the island: the American Sand Sculpting Championship runs through Monday behind DiamondHead Beach Resort, and the town has warned of traffic delays.
