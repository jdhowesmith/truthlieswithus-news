Will Cuylle scored twice, including the go-ahead short-handed goal late in the second period, and the New York Rangers rallied past the Montreal Canadiens 3-1 on Monday night, the Associated Press reported.

The Rangers have won three straight and six of their last seven, and they are 9-2-0 at home. The game followed a ceremony at Madison Square Garden marking the 100th anniversary of the first game in franchise history, which was against the Montreal Maroons.

## How it happened

Montreal controlled long stretches in the offensive zone and took the lead when Lane Hutson spun around Matt Rempe and fired a shot that Phillip Danault deflected in.

The lead lasted less than two minutes. Defenceman Marcus Pettersson banked the puck in off Cuylle in the crease to tie it.

Then the Canadiens' power play hurt them. After the top unit failed to score, Alex Newhook missed the net, and Cuylle raced the other way to beat goalie Jacob Fowler short side with 56.3 seconds left in the second period.

Another Montreal power play in the third was cut short by an interference penalty on Noah Dobson. Scott Morrow scored his second career goal with 13:16 left to seal it.

## In goal

Fowler allowed three goals on 26 shots. Joonas Korpisalo made 24 saves for New York and is 3-0 since returning from an injury that kept him out for the first five-plus weeks of the season.

**Next:** the Canadiens visit New Jersey on Wednesday. The Rangers host defending champion Carolina in a Sunday matinee.
