Brock Boeser scored a power-play goal with 4:23 left to give the Vancouver Canucks a 3-2 win over the Utah Mammoth on Monday night, the Associated Press reported. It was the first time this season Vancouver has won two games in a row.

Jake DeBrusk flicked the puck out to Boeser, who threaded it inside the post for the winner.

## Back-and-forth third period

Neither team scored until DeBrusk chipped in a power-play goal at the post with 4:55 left in the second period. He scored 10 seconds into the man advantage after Boeser sent the puck across to him.

Vincent Trocheck tied it for Utah on a power play at 2:51 of the third. Filip Chytil restored Vancouver's lead at 4:40, wrapping around the net and banking the puck in off goaltender Karel Vejmelka's heel. Barrett Hayton tied it again with 8:20 left before Boeser's winner.

## A strong debut in goal

Nikita Tolopilo, making his first start of the season, stopped 35 of 37 shots, according to the official NHL.com box score. (The AP game story listed 32 saves.) Vejmelka made 21 saves for Utah, which has lost six straight and is 10-9-3.

The Canucks improved to 5-17-1, still near the bottom of the league.

**Next:** Vancouver visits Seattle on Thursday. Utah hosts Colorado on Wednesday.
