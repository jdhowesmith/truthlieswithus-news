The City of Salmon Arm will ask the province for permission to install three red-light cameras on Highway 1 because semi-truck drivers keep running red lights, Castanet reports. Mayor Alan Harrison named the 4 Street NE intersection at the bottom of Tank Hill as a particular concern. RCMP can't watch the intersections 24 hours a day.

## Rural policing

The city is also asking for more rural policing. Harrison says rural RCMP resources in the area haven't increased since 2004, so city officers get pulled out to rural emergencies.

The issue came up at the Nov. 9 council meeting, after talks with the solicitor general at the UBCM convention.
