**Thursday update:** Gitxsan hereditary chiefs want trains stopped along the Skeena River until the line from Burns Lake to Prince Rupert is inspected, CBC reports. Tuesday's derailment was the second there in eight months. CN says diesel leaked from a locomotive and the two hospitalized employees have been released.

A CN Rail train derailed near Terrace in northwestern B.C. on Tuesday afternoon, sending several containers into the Skeena River and putting two people in hospital.

In a statement, CN said the derailment was caused by a rockslide, and that two locomotives and five rail cars carrying forest products left the tracks, CBC News reported. An earlier CN statement reported by The Canadian Press said preliminary reports indicated one locomotive and five railcars. It happened around 1:10 p.m., about 16 kilometres southwest of Terrace.

## Injuries

RCMP say the injuries are not life-threatening. B.C. Emergency Health Services described the two patients as being in "serious, but stable condition," according to CBC.

## Fuel in the river

CN confirmed diesel fuel leaked from a locomotive and said cleanup was underway. The company told The Canadian Press there was no fire, no dangerous goods were involved and there was no public safety risk.

## Highway 16

Emergency vehicles closed Highway 16 west of Terrace shortly after 1 p.m. as police, ambulance and fire crews responded, CBC reported. Check DriveBC for the latest on the highway.
