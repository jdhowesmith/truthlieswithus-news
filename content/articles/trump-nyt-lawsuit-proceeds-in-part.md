A federal judge has allowed parts of President Donald Trump's US$15-billion defamation lawsuit against the New York Times to go forward.

U.S. District Judge Steven Merryday let some claims proceed and dismissed others. The suit targets the Times, several of its reporters, and publisher Penguin Random House over the book *Lucky Loser* and related reporting.
