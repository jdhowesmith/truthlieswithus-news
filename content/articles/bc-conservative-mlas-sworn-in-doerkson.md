Sixty-four Conservative MLAs, including premier-designate Lorne Doerkson, take the oath of office at the B.C. legislature today, The Canadian Press reports.

Doerkson will be sworn in as British Columbia's 38th premier on Friday, along with his cabinet. NDP and Green MLAs are sworn in next week. The NDP now holds 27 of the legislature's 93 seats.
