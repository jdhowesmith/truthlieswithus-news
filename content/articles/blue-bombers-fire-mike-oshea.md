The Winnipeg Blue Bombers have fired head coach Mike O'Shea after 12 seasons, TSN and Sportsnet report.

O'Shea is the winningest coach in Blue Bombers history, at 126-76 in the regular season. He won Grey Cups in 2019 and 2021 and led Winnipeg to five straight Grey Cup appearances from 2019 to 2024. This season, Winnipeg lost the Eastern Final 33-23 to Montreal.
