## Shuswap

Environment Canada's Salmon Arm forecast calls for a mainly cloudy day with a 30% chance of flurries this morning, clearing this afternoon, with a high of +2°C. It was −0.9°C at Salmon Arm at 7 a.m. Tonight will be clear, then partly cloudy after midnight, with a low of −3°C.

Saturday is mainly cloudy, clearing near noon, high +2°C, low −4°C. Sunday turns cloudy with a 70% chance of flurries and a high of −1°C. Monday brings a mix of sun and cloud, high +2°C.

No weather alerts are in effect for the Shuswap or South Thompson.

## Fort Myers Beach

The National Weather Service forecast for Fort Myers Beach calls for a partly sunny day with a high near 79°F (26°C) and a light northwest wind. Tonight is partly cloudy with a low around 70°F (21°C) and a 20% chance of an isolated shower early in the evening.

Saturday is mostly sunny, high near 79°F (26°C), and Sunday sunny, high near 80°F (27°C).

There are no land or marine alerts for Lee County.
