The International Court of Justice ruled Thursday, by a vote of 9–6, that Myanmar violated the Genocide Convention in its 2017 military campaign in northern Rakhine state, according to CBC News and The Associated Press.

The court found that Myanmar's security forces killed thousands of Rohingya civilians (about 10,000, according to the evidence) and committed widespread sexual violence. It rejected Myanmar's argument that the campaign targeted insurgents.

This is the first time the court has ruled that a state committed genocide. Gambia brought the case in 2019.
