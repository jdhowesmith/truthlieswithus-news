Brooke Henderson shot a 2-over-par 74 in the first round of the CME Group Tour Championship at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Fla., and is tied for 57th in the 60-player field.

Henderson went out in 39 and came home in 35. Only Lydia Ko, at 77, is lower. Henderson is tied with Erika Hara and Jenny Shin, nine shots behind the leaders.

## The leaders

Defending champion Jeeno Thitikul, who birdied her last five holes, A Lim Kim, and Australian Karis Davidson, in her first CME start, share the lead at 7-under 65. Nine players are one back at 66, including Charley Hull and Ayaka Furue. Nelly Korda shot 68; a win would put her in the LPGA Hall of Fame. Forty-nine of the 60 players broke par.

## Round 2

Henderson teed off at 5:55 a.m. B.C. time in the first group, off the first tee, with Ko. Golf Channel coverage starts at noon B.C. time, after her round is likely to finish, so follow her on the LPGA leaderboard.
