Cadet units in the Shuswap will keep their weekly training nights, but extra activities are on hold from Nov. 21 to March 31 while the Canadian Armed Forces reviews its finances, the Salmon Arm Observer reports.

The local units are the 1787 Rocky Mountain Rangers army cadet corps in Salmon Arm, which has 18 cadets, and 222 Shuswap Air Cadets.
