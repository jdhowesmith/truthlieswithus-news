The Edmonton Oilers take the NHL's best record into Sunrise, Fla., tonight to face the rival Florida Panthers, looking for a sixth straight win and a piece of hockey history for Connor McDavid.

Puck drop at Amerant Bank Arena is 5 p.m. B.C. time. The game is on Sportsnet and TVA Sports in Canada and Scripps in the U.S.

## Edmonton on a roll

The Oilers are 16-3-3 with 35 points, first in the Pacific Division and first in the league. They won both weekend games, 1-0 over Toronto on Saturday behind Devon Levi's first career NHL shutout and 5-2 in Philadelphia on Sunday with Tristan Jarry making 32 saves.

Edmonton is 9-0-1 on the road. According to the Oilers, that makes them the 11th team in NHL history to open a season with a 10-game road point streak. Tonight is game three of a six-game road trip.

## McDavid and Gretzky

McDavid has a point in all 22 games this season, the longest point streak of his career, with 53 points (12 goals, 41 assists). He has already tied Dany Heatley for the fifth-longest season-opening point streak in NHL history. A point tonight would tie Wayne Gretzky's 23-game run from 1988-89 for fourth.

Leon Draisaitl leads the league with 20 goals and has eight in his last seven games.

## The Panthers

Florida is 12-7-3, holding the East's final wild-card spot, and lost 2-1 in overtime to Dallas on Sunday. Matthew Tkachuk is fifth in NHL scoring with 30 points and has points in eight straight games.

The Oilers' preview said captain Aleksander Barkov "could be poised" to return after missing 20 games. But Florida Hockey Now reported Tuesday morning that Barkov is not expected to play. Neither team had confirmed lineups early Tuesday. Brad Marchand is "getting closer," with no return date.

Florida has won five of the last six regular-season meetings, dating back to Nov. 20, 2023.

## Oilers notes

Ryan Nugent-Hopkins missed Sunday's game with what the team calls an undisclosed issue, and his status for tonight was unknown early Tuesday. Isaac Howard was activated off injured reserve Monday and sent to the AHL's Bakersfield Condors.

**Next up:** Thursday at Tampa Bay (5:30 p.m. B.C. time) and Saturday at Carolina (11 a.m. B.C. time).
