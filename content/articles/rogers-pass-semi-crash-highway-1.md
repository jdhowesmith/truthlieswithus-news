Drivers in Rogers Pass on Sunday ran into a closed westbound lane and debris across the highway after a semi-trailer rolled on a sharp bend.

The truck and its van trailer tipped over in the left lane on Nov. 15 while heading west past the Illecillewaet Campground in Glacier National Park, the Salmon Arm Observer reported. Freight spilled out the top and into the median ditch, but it did not block the eastbound lanes.

## Injuries and response

BC Highway Patrol arrived first, around 1 p.m. Sunday. An ambulance came from Golden, Revelstoke Fire Rescue Services sent an engine, and Parks Canada handled traffic control.

Firefighters extracted the driver, who had serious but non-life-threatening injuries and was airlifted out, according to the RCMP and BC Emergency Health Services. A second patient was treated by paramedics and left by ambulance in stable condition.

## Ten hours of delays

Westbound traffic was fully blocked for about three hours, until about 4 p.m., when traffic was switched to alternate in the eastbound lane. The westbound lanes reopened at 11:30 p.m., about 10 and a half hours after officers first responded, Revelstoke RCMP Staff Sgt. Chris Dodds told Black Press Media.

The crash is still under investigation by BC Highway Patrol. Dodds said "speed relative to conditions may have been a factor." Heading west from the summit, the posted limit drops from 70 to 50 km/h around the bend at Illecillewaet. The crash is not affecting traffic now.

## Shuswap roads this week

As of early Tuesday morning, DriveBC showed no crashes or unplanned closures on Highway 1 or Highways 97, 97A and 97B around the Shuswap. Things to know:

- **Sicamous:** Highway 1 between Old Sicamous Road and Solsqua-Sicamous Road is closed 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. on weekdays, including today. Use Highways 97A and 97B. Local access is allowed at the top of each hour.
- **Sicamous to Salmon Arm:** BC Hydro pole work means single-lane alternating traffic on weekdays, 8 a.m. to 4 p.m., until Nov. 30.
- **Fog:** patches on Highway 97A (Enderby to Sicamous), Highway 97B into Salmon Arm, and east of Sicamous toward Revelstoke.
- **Spallumcheen:** single-lane traffic on Highway 97 at Highland Road through Wednesday.
