Canada's Brooke Henderson begins play Thursday at the CME Group Tour Championship, the LPGA Tour's season finale at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Fla.

Henderson tees off the first hole at 11:05 a.m. ET (9:05 a.m. B.C. time), paired with Esther Henseleit of Germany, according to published first-round tee sheets. The LPGA's own tee-times page could not be checked this morning.

## The stakes

The tournament features the top players in the season-long Race to the CME Globe, competing for a top prize of $4 million out of an $11 million purse, Golf Channel reported.

## How to watch

In the U.S., the first round is live on Golf Channel and the Golf Channel app beginning at 2 p.m. ET Thursday (noon B.C. time), according to Golf Channel. TV coverage in Canada had not been confirmed as of Wednesday morning.

## Awards night

At the Rolex LPGA Awards in Naples on Tuesday night, Nelly Korda received the Rolex Player of the Year award, the LPGA said.
