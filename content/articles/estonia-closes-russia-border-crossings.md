Estonia will close all five of its border crossings with Russia for at least six months starting Dec. 1, citing security, Reuters reports.

The announcement came as Russia carried out one of the largest air attacks of the war on Ukraine overnight, and as Poland said Russian missiles and a drone crossed into its airspace.
