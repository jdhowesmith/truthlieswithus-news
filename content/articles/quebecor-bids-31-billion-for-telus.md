Quebecor, the Montreal-based owner of Freedom Mobile, announced Monday that it wants to buy Vancouver-based Telus, owner of Public Mobile, in a bid worth about $31 billion, CBC News reports. Quebecor says the deal would bring "pro-consumer pricing."

## The concern

Competition advocates told CBC that going from four big carriers to three would mean higher bills. It is the first major telecom merger proposal since Ottawa tightened competition rules after the Rogers–Shaw deal.

## B.C. reaction

Interim B.C. NDP Leader Jennifer Whiteside wrote to premier-designate Lorne Doerkson asking his government to oppose the bid, the Salmon Arm Observer reported.
