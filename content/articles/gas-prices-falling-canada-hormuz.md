Canada's national average gas price is $1.514 a litre today, according to affordableenergy.ca figures cited by CBC News. That's about 7 cents lower than a week ago and 28 cents lower than a month ago, and parts of Calgary are back below $1.

Analysts credit more tankers moving through the Strait of Hormuz under U.S. escorts, plus the seasonal switch to winter-blend fuel. Brent crude is just under US$92 a barrel, down from about US$116 in mid-October.

GasBuddy's Patrick De Haan told CBC he expects another 5 to 10 cents off in the coming weeks unless the Iran war flares up. Iran kept up attacks on tankers in the strait this week, the New York Times reported.
