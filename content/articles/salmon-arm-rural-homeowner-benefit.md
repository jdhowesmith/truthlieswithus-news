Salmon Arm city council will ask B.C.'s new government to restore the $200 Northern and Rural Home Owner Benefit, which the province plans to end on Jan. 1, 2027, the Salmon Arm Observer reports.

The benefit is paid on top of the $570 basic homeowner grant to eligible owners outside Metro Vancouver, Greater Victoria and the Fraser Valley. That includes homeowners across the Shuswap.

The request lands as Lorne Doerkson's Conservative government is sworn in today.
