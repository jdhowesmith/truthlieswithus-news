Lorne Doerkson becomes British Columbia's 38th premier today. He and his Conservative cabinet are to be sworn in at Government House in Victoria at 11 a.m. B.C. time.

On Thursday, 63 of the 64 Conservative MLAs took their oaths of office. The one still waiting is Kelowna Centre's Kristina Loewen, whose seat is going to a judicial recount; she leads by 43 votes.

## What happens next

Green MLAs are scheduled to be sworn in Nov. 23 and NDP MLAs on Nov. 26. Doerkson has said there will be no fall sitting of the legislature, so the new government's first session will come later.
