The U.S. Senate has passed a major permitting-reform bill by a vote of 62-33.

The Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act would speed up environmental reviews and permits for energy, mining and infrastructure projects. It would also expand federal authority over interstate power lines, and require large data centres to pay for the power infrastructure they need.

The bill now goes to the House of Representatives.
