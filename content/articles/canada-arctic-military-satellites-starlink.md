Canada is developing Arctic-ready military satellite communications to reduce its reliance on Elon Musk's Starlink network, CBC News reports.

Reliable communications across the Arctic are a long-standing challenge for the Canadian Armed Forces, and the move would give the military a Canadian-controlled option in the North.
