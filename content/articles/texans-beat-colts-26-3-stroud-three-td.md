The Houston Texans beat the Indianapolis Colts 26-3 on Thursday night to open Week 11 of the NFL season. ESPN said it was Houston's first home win of the season.

Indianapolis led 3-0 on Spencer Shrader's 34-yard field goal in the first quarter, then didn't score again. C.J. Stroud threw touchdown passes of 42 yards to Nico Collins and 24 yards to Kayshon Boutte. Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked a 27-yard field goal before halftime.

In the third quarter, Will Anderson Jr. sacked Daniel Jones in the end zone for a safety, and Stroud found Marlin Klein for a 13-yard touchdown.

Stroud finished 21-for-27 for 194 yards, three touchdowns and an interception. David Montgomery ran for 107 yards on 26 carries. Jones was 12-for-27 for 144 yards and an interception, and Jonathan Taylor was held to 21 yards on 11 carries.

Houston is 4-6 and Indianapolis is 3-8.
