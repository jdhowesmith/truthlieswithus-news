At Wednesday's Town of Fort Myers Beach code hearing, Magistrate Monica Schmucker fined the owners of 132 Andre Mar $82,000, Beach Talk Radio News reports. That fine keeps growing because the pool is still out of compliance, and the owners didn't show up.

The owners of 5850 Estero Blvd were fined $17,900 for a pool that was out of compliance for 114 days. Orders for several properties caught doing work without permits are coming within 15 days.
