Boaters around Fort Myers Beach should plan to stay in port for a few more days. The National Weather Service in Tampa Bay reissued its Small Craft Advisory at 4:33 a.m. Eastern Tuesday with stronger winds and rougher seas than before.

The advisory now calls for east winds of 20 to 30 knots with gusts to 35 knots and seas of 4 to 7 feet. Monday's version called for 20 to 25 knots, gusts to 30 and seas of 3 to 5 feet. It is in effect until 7 a.m. Eastern Friday, Nov. 20 (5 a.m. B.C. time), a little earlier than the previous end time.

It covers the coastal waters from Bonita Beach to Englewood, as well as Charlotte Harbor and Pine Island Sound and other Tampa Bay-area zones. The weather service says conditions are hazardous to small craft.

## The forecast on the island

- **Today:** Mostly cloudy with a 50% chance of showers, and 0.1 to 0.25 inches of rain possible. High near 79°F (26°C), falling to about 74°F (23°C) in the afternoon. East wind 15 to 20 mph, gusting to 29.
- **Tonight:** Showers likely (60%), with 0.5 to 0.75 inches possible. Low around 70°F (21°C).
- **Wednesday:** Showers likely in the morning, then a chance of showers and thunderstorms. High near 80°F (27°C).
- **Thursday:** Mostly sunny, high near 82°F (28°C), with a 30% chance of afternoon showers.

## At the beach

The rip current risk is low today, moderate tonight and low again Wednesday. Surf is a foot or less, and the water is in the mid-70s°F.

## No land alerts, quiet tropics

There are no flood, coastal flood, rip current or wind alerts for Lee County land areas. The National Hurricane Center says tropical cyclone formation is not expected in the Atlantic, Caribbean or Gulf over the next seven days.
