Showers and thunderstorms are likely at Fort Myers Beach today, and a small craft advisory remains in effect for local waters, although the National Weather Service has shortened it by a day.

## Today's forecast

The NWS Tampa Bay forecast for Fort Myers Beach, updated about 9:37 a.m. ET (7:37 a.m. B.C. time), calls for a chance of rain showers before 1 p.m. ET, then showers and thunderstorms likely. Skies will be mostly cloudy, with a high near 77°F (25°C) and an east wind around 17 mph, gusting to 24 mph. The chance of rain is 60%, with a quarter to half an inch possible.

Tonight, showers and storms are likely before 4 a.m. ET (2 a.m. B.C. time), with a low around 70°F (21°C).

Thursday turns sunny with a high near 80°F (27°C) and a 30% chance of afternoon showers. Friday is partly sunny, also near 80°F, with a 20% chance of showers.

## Marine: small craft advisory

NWS Tampa Bay reissued the small craft advisory at 3:54 a.m. ET Wednesday. It is now in effect **until 7 a.m. ET Thursday (5 a.m. B.C. time)**. The version issued Tuesday ran to Friday morning.

- **What:** East winds 20 to 25 knots with gusts up to 30 knots, and seas 4 to 7 feet.
- **Where:** Charlotte Harbor and Pine Island Sound, coastal waters from Bonita Beach to Englewood out 20 nautical miles, and waters from Bonita Beach to Englewood 20 to 60 nautical miles out.
- **Impacts:** "Conditions will be hazardous to small craft. Inexperienced mariners, especially those operating smaller vessels, should avoid navigating in hazardous conditions," the NWS said.

There were no NWS land alerts for Lee County as of about 8 a.m. B.C. time.

## Tropics

As of 7 a.m. ET Wednesday, the National Hurricane Center said a non-tropical low pressure system is expected to form near the northern Bahamas by this weekend. It has some potential for subtropical development while it moves north or northeast near or offshore of the southeastern U.S. coast. The chance of formation is near 0% over 48 hours and 20% over seven days, both rated low.
