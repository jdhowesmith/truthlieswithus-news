A Houthi missile attack Thursday damaged the tower housing Saudi Arabia's sovereign wealth fund, the Public Investment Fund, in Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District, which includes the city's tallest building, according to Reuters and the Wall Street Journal. The district was evacuated. It's the second strike on the district this month, after Aramco's building was hit Nov. 8. No casualties have been reported.

## Gaza

Israel's military says its ground operation in central Gaza, beyond the "yellow line," is completed after about 24 hours. It was the first incursion across that ceasefire line since the truce last October and forced dozens of displaced Palestinians to flee their tents, The Guardian and BBC report.
