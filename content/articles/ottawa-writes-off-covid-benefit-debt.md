The federal government wrote off about $4.5 billion in COVID-19 benefits owed by people later found to be ineligible, CBC News reported.

The amount was written off as "uncollectable" during the 2025-26 fiscal year, which ended March 31, according to the government's 2026 public accounts. The report called that a "significant increase" from $26 million in write-offs the year before.

## Who it involved

Employment and Social Development Canada told CBC News in a statement that most of its pandemic benefit write-offs "pertained to deceased or vulnerable populations."

## How much is still owed

The government had earlier said it paid a total of $14 billion in COVID-19 benefits to people who were not eligible because they did not meet income requirements, CBC reported. By November 2025, that figure had fallen to about $10 billion, and it nearly halved to about $5.56 billion by the end of July.

The agency said in September that the latest reduction was due to factors including changes in eligibility and write-offs.
