U.S. Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth signed a military cooperation deal in Caracas on Wednesday with Venezuela's interim government, led by Delcy Rodríguez, the BBC and CNN report. It comes less than a year after U.S. forces seized Nicolás Maduro. Few details have been released. Hegseth called Venezuela a "compliant security partner." Military ties were cut in 2005 under Hugo Chávez.

## Greenland

Denmark's parliament approved the U.S.–Greenland security deal 109–0 on Thursday. Greenland's parliament had already approved it unanimously earlier this month.

## Senegal

Dozens are missing after a migrant boat capsized off Yenne, south of Dakar, overnight Tuesday into Wednesday. One survivor said about 50 people were aboard, and one death is confirmed. UNHCR says nearly 600 people have died this year trying to reach the Canary Islands.
