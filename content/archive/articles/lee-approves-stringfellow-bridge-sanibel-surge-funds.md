Lee County commissioners on Tuesday approved $992,941 in disaster-recovery funds for the full replacement of the Stringfellow Road bridge in St. James City on Pine Island, Gulf Coast News reported.

The bridge was built in 1970 and has suffered decades of wear and damage from Hurricane Ian. "That one was built in 1970, so it's due for replacement at this point. It's also close to saltwater, so you get splash on concrete and it can cause some erosion of the concrete," Robert Price, Lee County's director of transportation, told Gulf Coast News. The contract includes contract administration, inspection and materials sampling and testing for the construction project.

Jim Byer, a longtime St. James City resident, recalled Ian's surge moving boats and cars and damaging houses. "The hurricanes do damage things over time," he said.

## Sanibel and Captiva

Commissioners also approved $806,929 for storm-surge resiliency improvements along Sanibel-Captiva Road and Captiva Drive. The project includes storm-surge protection measures, stormwater management improvements, a sewer force main relocation and structural hardening, according to Gulf Coast News.
