Valley cloud and fog patches are hanging over the Shuswap this morning, and temperatures will stay just above freezing through the week.

## The forecast

Environment Canada's Salmon Arm forecast calls for cloud today except mainly sunny skies above the valley cloud, with fog patches dissipating near noon. The high is 4°C, with a wind chill of −4 this morning and wind up to 15 km/h.

Tonight will be mainly cloudy, with fog patches developing near midnight and a low of −3°C (wind chill −5 overnight).

Thursday will be cloudy, becoming a mix of sun and cloud late in the morning, with a high of 3°C, then cloudy periods and a low of −3°C.

## No alerts for the Shuswap

There are no Environment Canada warnings or advisories for the Shuswap or South Thompson. The only alert in B.C. this morning is an air quality warning for Stuart–Nechako, far to the north.

## Sun Peaks waits for snow

Sun Peaks Resort has pushed its opening day to Thursday, Nov. 26, at the earliest, Castanet reported. It had planned to open Saturday, Nov. 21, but mild weather has left too little snow.

Guests who bought lift tickets, rentals or lessons for the affected dates will get automatic refunds. Those with accommodation bookings should contact their lodging provider. It is the third straight year Sun Peaks has postponed its planned opening because of unfavourable weather, Castanet said.

## Alert test today

The provincewide test of the BC Emergency Alert system is scheduled for **1:55 p.m. today**, the B.C. government says. Alerts will be sent to cellphones and will interrupt TV and radio broadcasts. It is only a test.
