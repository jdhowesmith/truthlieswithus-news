The U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention says 148 people, 134 passengers and 14 crew, have reported vomiting and diarrhea on Princess Cruises' Caribbean Princess, according to Fox News and USA Today. The 12-night round trip left Fort Lauderdale Nov. 11 and returns Nov. 23. The cause isn't known yet, and tests are pending.

## Measles

Pennsylvania confirmed its eighth measles-related death, with cases in its outbreak now above 1,500, Reuters reports.
