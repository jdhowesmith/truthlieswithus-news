The Los Angeles Chargers finally won a football game, and they did it the hard way. Justin Herbert and Omarion Hampton ran for first-half touchdowns, and the Chargers dominated the line of scrimmage in a 23-20 win over the Baltimore Ravens on Monday night, the Associated Press reported.

Los Angeles outrushed Baltimore 160-16. Ravens star Derrick Henry was held to 10 yards on 10 carries, though he scored both Baltimore touchdowns.

"Let's do it every week," Chargers coach Jim Harbaugh said. "It was good to see the smiles back on the faces."

## How it unfolded

Herbert's 22-yard touchdown run on the Chargers' first possession gave them a lead they never gave up. After Henry's 2-yard run cut it to 10-7, Hampton was stopped at the 1 on fourth down by linebacker Roquan Smith but pushed into the end zone with help from teammates, making it 17-7 with 1:56 left in the half.

Henry's 1-yard run made it 23-20 with 10:23 to play. With 2:03 left, Zay Flowers lost a fumble near the Chargers' 45 after a catch. Tarheeb Still forced it and Elijah Molden recovered. Herbert then hit former Raven Charlie Kolar for a first down that all but ended the game.

Herbert completed only 12 passes for 129 yards, but it was his first game this season without an interception. He was sacked just twice after averaging nearly four sacks a game, and he ran for 51 yards on seven carries.

## Ravens in trouble

Baltimore fell to 4-6 with its fifth straight loss. Jacoby Brissett threw for 340 yards in his first game for the Ravens, who traded for him with Lamar Jackson and Tyler Huntley both on injured reserve. The Ravens went three-and-out on their first three drives and were booed at home, where they have lost seven of their last eight games.

"We have a two-game stretch before our bye to stay alive," Ravens coach Jesse Minter said.

The Chargers lost cornerback Rodney Shelley to an ankle injury. The Ravens lost guard Vega Ioane (shoulder) and defensive tackle Nnamdi Madubuike (hamstring).

**Next:** the Chargers host the New York Jets on Sunday; the Ravens visit Carolina.
