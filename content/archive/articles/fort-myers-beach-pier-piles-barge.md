Construction crews are driving production piles for the new Fort Myers Beach Pier, and Lee County officials are asking the public to take care near the work site.

A barge is now on site to support construction, and safety buoys will be positioned around the marine work area, according to the county notice published by Fort Myers Beach Talk. Work may also take place on weekends.

## On the water

Boaters, personal watercraft operators and swimmers should stay outside the marked area and avoid approaching the barge or the pier construction zone, officials said.

## On the beach

Lynn Hall Memorial Park remains closed for the pier reconstruction. Pedestrians and beachgoers should stay outside the construction fence, barriers and restricted work areas, and follow posted detour signs and designated crossings. County officials warned the public not to use construction entrances to get to the beach.

## Timeline

The pier work is expected to be completed in September, the county said. Lee County is also rebuilding a restroom facility at an elevated height at Lynn Hall Memorial Park, which is expected to be finished by April.
