House Democrats nominated Hakeem Jeffries for speaker on Wednesday in a secret ballot, 251 to 11, according to vote counts from NBC News' sources. There was no challenger. If he wins the full House vote in January, he would be the first Black speaker. Seven of the 11 who voted no said their talks with him are "ongoing."

## Alaska House seat

Republican Rep. Nick Begich won re-election Wednesday through ranked-choice tabulation, defeating independent Bill Hill, who was backed by the Alaska Democratic Party. Democrats won Alaska's Senate and governor's races, NBC News and Fox News report.

## Trump's arch

U.S. District Judge Tanya Chutkan extended her order blocking construction of Trump's planned 250-foot arch in Washington by 14 days, to at least Dec. 3, while she considers lawsuits by D.C. and Virginia seeking a longer block.
