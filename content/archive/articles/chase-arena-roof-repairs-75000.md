On the roof of Chase's Art Holding Memorial Arena, screws have come loose and the gutters are wrecked, so water is getting in, the Salmon Arm Observer reports.

Village council awarded the repair job to ArmourGrade Contracting for $74,147 plus GST. Its bid wasn't the lowest, but it came with a 20-year warranty. Work could start within a week.
