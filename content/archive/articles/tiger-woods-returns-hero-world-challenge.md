Tiger Woods will play in the Hero World Challenge next month, his first competitive start in more than two years, the PGA Tour announced Tuesday.

The 50-year-old 15-time major champion hosts the event, which runs Dec. 3-6 in New Providence, Bahamas, the Los Angeles Times reported. It will be his first tournament since he missed the cut at the 2024 Open Championship at Royal Troon.

## The field

Woods was one of three sponsor exemptions announced Tuesday morning, with Rickie Fowler and Gary Woodland rounding out the 20-player field, according to the PGA Tour. Hideki Matsuyama returns to defend his title, and world No. 1 Scottie Scheffler is also in the field.

## Two years away

Since his last start, Woods has had surgery on his back and an Achilles injury. The PGA Tour said he has had three surgeries in that time, two on his back and one on his Achilles. He played in TGL's first season in 2025.

His comeback was most recently delayed after he crashed his vehicle in March and was arrested, the PGA Tour said. In September, Woods pleaded guilty to reckless driving and refusing a lawful urinalysis test connected to the crash, the LA Times reported.

Woods has returned to the Hero World Challenge several times to test his game and fitness, most recently in 2024, according to the PGA Tour.
