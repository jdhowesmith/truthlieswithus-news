Amendment 3 is projected to cost the City of Cape Coral $42.3 million in fiscal 2028, which starts Oct. 1, 2027, the Cape Coral Breeze reports. Council will hold a meeting on it Dec. 16.

Council also approved a $400-million trash contract with FCC after a tense meeting, WINK News reports, and Rick Erickson was sworn in as mayor. Councilmember Rachel Kaduk was found not guilty at her DUI trial, Gulf Coast News reports.

## Elsewhere in Lee County

A man is suing the Lee County Sheriff's Office over an attack in a jail cell that left him with severe injuries. The suit was filed about a year after the beating, according to Gulf Coast News and the News-Press.
