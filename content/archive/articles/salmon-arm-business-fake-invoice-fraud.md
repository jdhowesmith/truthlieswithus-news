A Salmon Arm business lost nearly $25,000 after receiving a fraudulent invoice that appeared to come from a trusted supplier, according to RCMP.

Police said the business reported the $24,299 loss on Oct. 15, after making a payment using new banking information on what appeared to be a legitimate invoice, Castanet reported.

"The company received what they believed to be an invoice from a supplier they were very familiar with, but the invoice had new account, transit and institution numbers to make the deposit into," RCMP Const. Andrew Hodges said.

## Where the money went

Investigators traced the payment to a bank account that may have been used to move the stolen money without the account holder's knowledge.

"Police attended the bank of the account holder and learned scammers were possibly funneling the stolen funds through a bank account without the actual bank account owner's knowledge," Hodges said.

The incident remains under investigation. RCMP pointed residents and businesses to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre for information on common fraud schemes.
