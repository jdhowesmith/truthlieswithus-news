A federal jury in Miami found Christian Emmanuel Sanon, a dual Haitian-American citizen and Florida pastor, guilty on all eight charges after a nearly eight-week trial, The Guardian and Fox News report. The charges include conspiring to kill Haitian President Jovenel Moïse, who was assassinated in 2021.

Prosecutors said the plotters first wanted to install Sanon as president, and that the plot was partly financed with U.S. COVID relief loans. He faces up to life in prison at sentencing Feb. 10.
