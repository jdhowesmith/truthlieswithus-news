Connor McDavid had a goal and an assist to extend his season-opening point streak to 23 games, and the Edmonton Oilers beat the Florida Panthers 5-2 on Tuesday night in Sunrise, Fla.

The Oilers won their sixth straight game and have won nine of their last 11. They are 17-3-3, first in the Pacific Division and first in the NHL. The Panthers fell to 12-8-3, ending a four-game point streak, in a rematch of the last two Stanley Cup Finals, both won by Florida.

## McDavid and Gretzky

McDavid's streak ties Wayne Gretzky's 1988-89 run for the fourth-longest from the start of a season in NHL history. According to NHL.com, only Gretzky (51 games in 1983-84 and 30 in 1982-83) and Mats Sundin (30 in 1992-93) have had longer ones.

His power-play goal also gave him 441 power-play points (101 goals, 340 assists), passing Gretzky's 439 for the most in franchise history. It was McDavid's first power-play goal of the season.

"Connor is an unbelievable player," Oilers coach Mike Babcock said, according to NHL.com. "A spectacular talent. His drivetrain and his push for excellence is the thing that impresses me more than the talent."

Florida's Matthew Tkachuk agreed. "McDavid's controlling every single game he's playing right now," he said. "He's on a whole different level than anyone else in the League right now."

## How it happened

- **1st period, 9:57:** Kasperi Kapanen scored after a Mattias Ekholm shot deflected off Florida's Niko Mikkola. Oilers 1-0.
- **1st period, 16:27 (power play):** McDavid pulled in a rebound at the right post and went high on Jacob Markstrom. 2-0.
- **2nd period, 8:08:** Vasily Podkolzin finished a 3-on-2 set up by Draisaitl and Kapanen. 3-0.
- **2nd period, 14:23 (power play):** Podkolzin one-timed a Draisaitl pass. 4-0.
- **3rd period, 13:51 and 16:50:** Tkachuk scored twice for Florida. 4-2.
- **3rd period, 19:27:** Draisaitl scored into an empty net. 5-2 final.

The Oilers went 2-for-4 on the power play. Florida outshot Edmonton 22-18, according to the NHL box score.

## Lineup change paid off

Babcock moved Draisaitl off McDavid's wing to centre Podkolzin and Kapanen, with Ryan Nugent-Hopkins out for a second straight game with what the team calls an undisclosed issue. All three finished with three points. Draisaitl's assist on Podkolzin's first goal extended his point streak to 10 games, the 12th time in his career he has done that, NHL.com said.

## Levi keeps winning

Devon Levi made 20 saves and is 10-1-0. His bid for a second straight shutout ended with 6:19 left. NHL.com said he reached 10 wins with Edmonton in fewer games than any Oiler before him. The Oilers are 10-0-1 on the road, and NHL.com said they are the fifth team in league history to win 10 road games in 11 or fewer.

**Next up:** Thursday at the Tampa Bay Lightning (5:30 p.m. B.C. time), then Saturday at Carolina (11 a.m. B.C. time).
