Brooke Henderson tees off at 9:05 a.m. B.C. time (11:05 a.m. ET) in the first round of the LPGA's season-ending CME Group Tour Championship at Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Fla. She is paired with Esther Henseleit of Germany off the 1st tee, according to Golf Post and Golf Channel.

The field is 60 players, with no cut, an $11-million purse and $4 million to the winner.

## Where she stands

Henderson came in 19th in the Race to CME Globe and is No. 12 in the Rolex world rankings. This is her 12th straight CME Group Tour Championship, according to Golf Canada, and she tied for 7th here last year. Golf Canada says her 2027 status was already secure after she finished runner-up at the Evian and tied for third at the KPMG Women's PGA in 2026.

## Storylines

Nelly Korda leads the Race to CME Globe by 600 points, Golfweek reports. Defending champion Jeeno Thitikul has won this event the last two years.

## How to watch

Golf Channel airs the tournament from 1 to 3 p.m. B.C. time Thursday and Friday, with the Golf Channel app streaming from noon. Saturday is on CNBC from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m., and Sunday on NBC from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. B.C. time. In Canada, check your Golf Channel Canada listings.
