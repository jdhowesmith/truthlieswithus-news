The grandstand at Sorrento-Blind Bay Community Park will close for the winter starting Thursday, Nov. 19, the Columbia Shuswap Regional District (CSRD) announced.

The decision followed an engineering assessment that raised concerns about the structure's ability to safely support snow loads, the CSRD said in a news release posted Tuesday.

## A second closure this year

The grandstand was first closed to the public on April 30, after engineers developing plans to replace the roof discovered structural instability. In June, a temporary brace was designed and installed so the grandstand could be used safely during the summer. The CSRD said the bracing was only intended as a short-term measure.

## What's next

The grandstand will remain closed until further notice. Because repairs will have budget implications, the project will be considered as part of the CSRD's 2027 budget process, the regional district said.

The rest of the park is not mentioned in the closure notice.
