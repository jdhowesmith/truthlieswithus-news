Bryson DeChambeau is joining the DP World Tour for 2027 with full "category one" membership, so he can enter any event, according to BBC Sport and AAP. The tour announced it Tuesday. DeChambeau has said he'll play the Nedbank Golf Challenge in South Africa in December. The top 10 on the DP World Tour standings earn PGA Tour cards.

Nine other former LIV players get a limited number of invitations, excluding Rolex Series events: Dustin Johnson, Cameron Smith, Bubba Watson, Lucas Herbert, Abraham Ancer, Joaquin Niemann, Carlos Ortiz, Thomas Pieters and Michael La Sasso. Jon Rahm and Tyrrell Hatton return full-time.

"We realise there are different viewpoints. But this approach... will add value which, in turn, will benefit the tour as a whole," tour CEO Guy Kinnings said, as quoted by BBC Sport.

The LIV bankruptcy hearing is scheduled for Tuesday, Nov. 24.
