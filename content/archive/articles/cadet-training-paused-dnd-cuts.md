Most cadet weekend training and competitions are halted until March 31, 2027, because of the Department of National Defence's operations and maintenance cuts, CBC News reports. Weekly parade nights continue for now.

DND says funding for cadets and junior rangers is under review. The Ottawa Citizen reported the pause first, and CBC confirmed it with leaked emails.
