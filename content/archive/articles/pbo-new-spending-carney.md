The parliamentary budget officer is questioning the federal government's vow to spend less and invest more.

In a series of reports released Tuesday scrutinizing the federal budget, PBO Annette Ryan projects that the cost of federal measures is $331.6 billion higher across the three spending plans tabled so far under Prime Minister Mark Carney, The Canadian Press reported.

## Operations, not capital

Most of that spending is for the day-to-day operations of government, not the kind of capital spending Carney has promised to prioritize, according to the PBO.

## Revenue room spent

Ryan said the government's overall fiscal situation is a bit better than forecast in Ottawa's spring spending update. But she said the government has used up 96% of the new fiscal room from higher-than-expected revenue through fresh spending commitments, instead of reducing the deficit.

While the government laid out a path to meet its fiscal anchors in Budget 2026, the PBO warns those margins are narrow.
