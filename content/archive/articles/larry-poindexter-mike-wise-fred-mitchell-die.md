## Larry Poindexter, 66

Veteran character actor Larry Poindexter, with almost 200 film and TV credits, died Wednesday morning, Nov. 18, in hospital while waiting for surgery on broken ribs, Deadline reports. He was hit by a car Friday night, Nov. 13, while crossing a street in Studio City, Los Angeles. No official cause of death has been released.

He was best known as LAPD Capt. Tom Fuller opposite Samuel L. Jackson in *S.W.A.T.* (2003), and also appeared in *Days of Our Lives*, *General Hospital*, *Santa Barbara* and *JAG*.

## Mike Wise, 62

Mike Wise, a prize-winning sports columnist for The Washington Post who also wrote for The New York Times, helped build ESPN's *The Undefeated* and won an Emmy as a TV reporter, died unexpectedly Saturday, Nov. 14, in Panama, where he lived with his family, according to a close friend's post cited by the Post. No cause was given.

## Fred Mitchell, 78

Fred Mitchell, the Chicago Tribune's first Black sportswriter, worked at the paper for 41 years and later taught. His death was reported Wednesday by the Tribune and the Chicago Sun-Times.
