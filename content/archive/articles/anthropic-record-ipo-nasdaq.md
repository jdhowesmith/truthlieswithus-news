Anthropic, the maker of the Claude chatbot, begins trading on Nasdaq today after pricing its initial public offering at US$83 a share, above its marketed range of US$70 to US$78, according to CBC News and the New York Times.

That values the company at about US$1.51 trillion. The offering raised about US$81 billion, a record. CBC notes the raise is more than every major Canadian IPO of the past 30 years combined.
