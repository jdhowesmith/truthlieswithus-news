The National Hurricane Center's 7 a.m. ET outlook (5 a.m. B.C. time) gives a system off the Southeast U.S. coast a 20% chance of development in 48 hours and 60% (medium) in seven days. Yesterday it was near 0% and 20%.

A non-tropical low is expected to form along a front near the northwest Bahamas on Friday. It could become a subtropical or tropical storm as it moves north or northeast near or just off the southeastern U.S. coast through the weekend. NHC says that whether or not it develops, strong winds, dangerous surf and coastal flooding are likely along parts of the Southeast and mid-Atlantic coasts into early next week. The outlook area runs from Florida's Atlantic side northward and does not include Southwest Florida.

## Fort Myers Beach

The National Weather Service forecasts mostly sunny skies and a high near 81°F (27°C) today, with a 20% chance of a shower after 1 p.m. ET. Friday and Saturday look similar, with highs near 80°F (27°C). The rip current risk is low today, tonight and Friday, with surf 1 foot or less. Yesterday's Small Craft Advisory ended at 7 a.m. ET, and there are no land alerts for Lee County. Florida's east coast is a different story: NWS has flood watches for Palm Beach, Broward and Miami-Dade counties and much of Central Florida, plus flash flood warnings in Brevard County.

## Shuswap

Environment Canada's Salmon Arm forecast calls for mainly cloudy skies clearing late this morning, with a high of +3°C and a low of −3°C tonight. Friday and Saturday bring sun and highs near +1°C to +2°C. Snow is forecast for Sunday. There are no alerts for the Shuswap or South Thompson.

On the roads, DriveBC reports fog patches on Highway 1 between Waverly Park and Bernie Road and on Highway 97B into Salmon Arm. Highway 1 between Old Sicamous Road and Solsqua-Sicamous Road is closed 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. on weekdays, including today and Friday; use Highways 97A and 97B. Rogers Pass has no closures listed this morning.
