The American Sand Sculpting Championship starts today on Fort Myers Beach. It runs Thursday to Sunday at the DiamondHead Beach Resort, with an extra public viewing day Monday, Fort Myers Beach Talk reports. About 30 professional sculptors are taking part, and this year's theme is "Stars, Stripes & Sand" for the U.S. 250th anniversary. The Chamber calls it the unofficial kickoff of the season.

WINK News warns of traffic delays on the island during the event.

## Also on the beach

Kilwins is back. The fudge and ice cream shop, whose Times Square store was destroyed by Hurricane Ian, opened Monday under new franchise owners at a new spot on Old San Carlos Boulevard, Fort Myers Beach Talk reports.
