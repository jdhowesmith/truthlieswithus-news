The Dallas Cowboys are taking defensive playcalling away from coordinator Christian Parker and giving it to senior defensive assistant Mike Pettine for the rest of the season, sources told ESPN.

Parker, 34, will remain on the staff as defensive coordinator. ESPN reported the organization does not want to give up on the promise it believes he has.

## The numbers

Through 10 games, the Cowboys are last in the NFL in total yards allowed (393.8 a game), last in run defence (154.1) and last in third-down defence (48.8%), according to ESPN.

In Sunday's 46-40 loss to the San Francisco 49ers, Dallas gave up 584 yards, the most in a home game in team history and the third most in any game. The Cowboys have allowed more than 20 points in every game this season, a streak that has reached 17 games over two seasons. They have also given up a league-high eight plays of 50 yards or more, six in the past three games.

## Who is Pettine

Pettine joined the Cowboys before Week 5 to help Parker, whom he brought into the NFL with the Green Bay Packers in 2019. He has been a defensive coordinator with the New York Jets, Buffalo Bills and Packers, and was head coach of the Cleveland Browns in 2014 and 2015, ESPN reported.

Parker became the youngest defensive coordinator in team history when he was hired in January, after two seasons as the Philadelphia Eagles' defensive passing game coordinator.

## No NFL games Tuesday

There were no NFL games on Tuesday. Week 11 begins Thursday night with the Indianapolis Colts at the Houston Texans.
